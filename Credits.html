Created by José Manuel García (JM.G)

ZuckerMixer was made to mix the ZuckerSession multitrack jam recordings. It finds the songs in a long session, balances every instrument, adds effects and masters each song to MP3, all on your own computer: your recordings never leave it.

ZuckerMixer is free software under the GNU General Public License v3.0.
Source code, releases and issue reports: github.com/amehcgracia-byte/ZuckerMixer

Built with open-source tools: Matchering (reference mastering), FFmpeg (audio encoding), faster-whisper (song detection), NumPy and SciPy (signal processing), Flask and pywebview (the app window).
